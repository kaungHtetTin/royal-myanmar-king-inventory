# Sales Representative Guide

## Start the route

1. Sign in at `/sales/login`; confirm your name, stock, pending receiving, cash hold, and Online state.
2. Receive only transfers issued to you after physically checking the products and quantities.
3. Report differences before receiving; version 1 does not support partial receipt.

## Create a sale

1. Open New sale and choose an active customer.
2. Add products from your available stock. Prices and totals are calculated by the server.
3. Choose Cash or Credit. Credit is allowed only when the customer setting and remaining limit permit it.
4. Save the draft, review customer/payment/products/quantities/total, then post once. Do not refresh or repeat the action while it is processing.
5. Confirm the posted reference in History. Posted sales cannot be edited.

## Stock and cash

- My stock is read-only and must match physical custody. Report discrepancies to the office for an authorized adjustment process.
- Cash sales increase your cash hold. Create a cash submission only for money physically handed to the office.
- A pending submission does not reduce cash hold; office confirmation does. Keep the reference as handover evidence.

## Offline/error behavior

The application may show a previously loaded read-only shell while offline. It does not queue sales, receiving, or cash submissions. When the message “Internet connection is required to complete this transaction.” appears, restore a reliable connection and verify current balances before trying once. Give support the reference, time, and request ID where available—never your password.
